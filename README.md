> **"A lightweight, ad-free software station delivering only the tools you truly need."**

---

## 📌 Who We Are / Our Philosophy
ZuruPrograms believes that software should reach the user in its purest and most useful form, without being bloated. **Whatever we learned from the Linux legends**, we reflect directly into our projects!

*   ⚡ **Lightweight & Performant:** We stay away from bloated codebases, creating minimalist structures that won't lag your device.
*   🛡️ **Ad-Free & Clean:** Our apps do not contain annoying ads or useless plugins that drag down performance.
*   🌐 **Completely Free (as in Freedom):** We don't hide our code; we share it, improve it, and distribute it freely under GPLv3.
